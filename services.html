Virtual Home Schooling (Nursery – SSS3)

Detailed Explanation: A complete, strictly virtual K–12 institution designed for Nigerian families who want quality, deliberate education without the limitations of physical schools. Covers all core subjects through a team of seasoned teachers. Blends live instruction (via free tools like WhatsApp groups, Zoom, Google Meet), recorded lessons, structured LMS platforms (Junior Class & Senior Class), and strong parent accountability.

Curriculum Structure by Level

  • Nursery & Primary: Foundational literacy, numeracy, basic science, social studies, creative arts. Play-based + structured.
  • Junior Secondary (JSS1–JSS3): Full Nigerian curriculum via Junior Class LMS + live support. BECE/Junior WAEC prep.
  • Senior Secondary (SSS1–SSS3): Science, Arts, Commercial streams. WAEC, NECO, GCE, UTME, IGCSE, IELTS, JUPEB, SAT focus. Senior Class LMS + exam clinics.

Parent Accountability & Dashboard

Parents receive weekly progress reports, access to a simulated dashboard (see full interactive demo on the Virtual Home Schooling page), mastery certificates, and direct WhatsApp channel to teachers. No surprises — full visibility.

Technology Stack (Free Only)

Junior/Senior Class LMS platforms (browser-based), localStorage for progress, WhatsApp for live sessions & support, recorded videos hosted on free platforms, study plan generators. No paid AI APIs — cost-effective and scalable for families and schools.

Enterprise / School Partnership Angle

Schools can adopt the full Virtual Home Schooling model as a hybrid or fully virtual arm. Includes teacher training, curriculum mapping, and InstaDocs for report cards/certificates.

CBT Pro (Free for Schools)

Detailed Explanation: The flagship free Computer-Based Testing platform built by a tutor, for tutors. Created entirely on an Android tablet (Acode editor) with ₦0 budget to prove that constraints are not blockers — clarity of thought is. Now live in real Nigerian secondary school classrooms.

Core Features (8+)
  • ✓ 7-column CSV question upload (no manual entry)
  • ✓ Timed exams with auto-submit
  • ✓ Per-student question & option randomisation (anti-cheat)
  • ✓ Tab-switch detection with configurable tolerance
  • ✓ Flag-for-review
  • ✓ Auto-marking + instant WhatsApp-shareable certificates
  • ✓ 4-tab teacher analytics (Overview, Question Analysis, Student Comparison, Trends)
  • ✓ Optional Gemini AI explanations (free tier only when needed)
Enterprise & Free Stack Details

Built with HTML5 + Vanilla JS + Supabase (free tier with Row-Level Security). No paid AI required for core use. Deployable for entire schools at zero cost. Full documentation and teacher training included in our 5-step process.
