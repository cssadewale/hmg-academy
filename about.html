The Story Behind HMG Academy

Not a marketing page. A real story about 15+ years in Nigerian classrooms, a long-held dream, an Android tablet — and what happens when an educator who always wanted to solve classroom problems with technology finally finds the tools to do it.

WHO RUNS HMG ACADEMY

One Person. Two Callings.

Adewale Samson Adeagbo is not a data scientist who happens to teach. He is not a teacher who dabbles in tech. He is both — deliberately, simultaneously, and by design.

🏫
The Educator — 15+ Years

Adewale began teaching in 2009 and has been in Nigerian secondary school classrooms every year since. He teaches Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry from JSS1 to SSS3. He has served as Head Teacher and Principal, counselled students, registered thousands for national exams, and built HMG Academy as a full virtual school with a team of seasoned teachers covering all subjects and all major Nigerian and international examinations.

  • • Mathematics, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry (personal specialist subjects)
  • • HMG Academy covers ALL subjects through our experienced teacher team
  • • WAEC · NECO · GCE · BECE · UTME · Post-UTME
  • • International: IGCSE · IELTS · JUPEB · SAT
  • • Head Teacher / Principal — High Favour School (2015–2019)
  • • Founder & Director — HMG Concepts (est. 2015)
📊
The AI-Augmented Solutions Developer

Adewale's formal tech journey began with the 3MTT programme in 2025 — the turning point that transformed 15+ years of classroom observations into deployable technical solutions. He is a data scientist who uses AI fluency to build solutions in any field — not just education. 34+ projects deployed. 7 industries served.

  • • Data Scientist — 10+ deployed ML models and EdTech tools
  • • AI-Augmented Solutions Developer — builds in new fields using AI as force multiplier
  • • CBT Pro — live exam platform used by real students (built on Android tablet, zero budget)
  • • 3MTT · WorldQuant University · DSN × Google · SkillBuild Hub Ambassador
  • • B.Sc.(Ed) Computer Science Education — LASU (2023)
  • • Philosophy: “Think clearly. Build anyway.”
THE REAL STORY

Why HMG Academy Exists

2009
First Classroom
Adewale begins teaching in Lagos secondary schools. He immediately notices the gap between what students need and what the available tools offer — manual processes, paper-based everything, no reliable data about student performance.
2015
HMG Concepts Founded
His Marvellous Grace Educational Consult is established — starting as a tutoring service, growing into the multi-subsidiary educational organisation it is today. HMG Academy, HMG Technologies, and HMG Media are born under this umbrella.
2019
The University Decision
After 10 years in classrooms, Adewale returns to tertiary education — enrolling at Lagos State University to study Computer Science Education. The specific ambition: to build the technical foundation needed to solve classroom and school problems with technology.
2023
B.Sc.(Ed) Completed
Graduates from LASU with a degree in Computer Science Education. The foundation is laid — but the full problem-solving clarity he sought does not arrive immediately at graduation.
2025 — THE TURNING POINT
3MTT Changes Everything
Adewale joins the Federal Government's 3MTT programme — Data Science track. Something unlocks. His inquisitiveness deepens. The classroom problems he had been observing for 16 years suddenly have clear technical pathways. CBT Pro launches. The ML projects begin. The AI-Augmented Solutions Developer identity takes shape.
2026 — Now
HMG Academy — Fully Live
HMG Academy operates as a full virtual school. 34+ projects deployed. DSN × Google, SkillBuild Ambassador, WorldQuant University. Every project documented. The journey is public — and honest. All four HMG arms are now live.
