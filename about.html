Detailed philosophy

“Learning Deliberately. Teaching Authentically.” means HMG Academy does not chase technology hype. Every feature must solve a learner, parent, tutor or school problem. AI is a force multiplier for clear thinking, not a replacement for educators. Paid AI APIs are avoided because recurring costs can exclude schools and students. The rebuild therefore uses static files, browser storage, WhatsApp, free hosting, open web standards and the existing HMG ecosystem tools.
