The Honest Version

What Nobody Usually Says

I started HMG Academy in 2015 because I believed students in Nigerian secondary schools deserved more than rote learning and exam pressure. Ten years on, that belief is the same — but the tools have changed dramatically.

The path into data science wasn't glamorous. There was imposter syndrome — still is, honestly. There were nights learning Pandas after full teaching days. There was building CBT Pro on a tablet because that's what I had. But every project, every model, every notebook I shipped publicly was both proof of work and a lesson in itself.

I approach data science the same way I approach maths teaching: from first principles, step by step, with no shortcuts. If a student can't follow it, the explanation isn't good enough yet. That philosophy shapes every notebook I write and every tool I build.

"Data science taught me that if you can think through a problem clearly, you can find a path to a solution — even in unfamiliar territory. That is the same lesson I bring to every student I teach."

HMG Academy is the intersection of both worlds: a teaching brand that builds technology, and a technology brand that never forgets its students. Everything here — the CBT platform, the performance trackers, the ML models — was made to solve real problems I encountered in real classrooms.
