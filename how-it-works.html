How it works

Six steps from confusion to measurable progress.

01

Insights Assessment

We start with diagnostics, parent/student interview, CBT or subject checks to identify level and learning gaps.

02

Adaptive Learning Plan

We build a plan using HMG LMS, past questions, weekly milestones and learner constraints.

03

Vetted Tutor Match

Tutors are filtered by subject mastery, teaching style, availability, language and safety requirements.

04

Live Virtual Learning

Lessons happen online using free meeting tools, whiteboard workflows, shared files and structured assignments.

05

Evaluation & Dashboard

Progress is tracked with quizzes, CBT Pro, milestones, local dashboard demos and tutor feedback.

06

Report, Review & Uplift

Parents/schools receive clear reports, next actions, recommendations and updated learning plans.
