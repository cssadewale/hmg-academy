Frequently Asked Questions

Is everything really free for students and schools?
Core learner access to CBT Pro, all 11 LMS platforms, and basic Virtual Home Schooling resources is free. Some advanced enterprise features or custom school rollouts may have nominal support fees. We are committed to ₦0 core access.
Do you use paid AI APIs?
No. We deliberately avoid paid AI APIs because they are not cost-effective for Nigerian schools and students. We use free tools (SQL.js, localStorage, rule-based systems, open models when needed) so tools remain accessible and scalable.
How does Virtual Home Schooling work?
Live sessions via WhatsApp + free video tools, structured curriculum in our Junior/Senior Class LMS, weekly plans, parent dashboard (demo on the page), mastery certificates. Full Nigerian curriculum + international exam prep.
Can schools deploy CBT Pro and the LMS for free?
Yes. CBT Pro is completely free for schools. LMS platforms are free for learners. We provide full training and documentation using our 5-step "How We Work" process.
Where are all the projects hosted?
CBT Pro on Vercel, LMS on Vercel, ML models and tools on Streamlit Community Cloud, simulators on Vercel/GitHub Pages. All free hosting. Links are the exact live versions.
