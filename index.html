How HMG works

Assessment → Tutor → Lessons → Progress → Results.

We do not just connect learners to tutors. We assess the learner, create a learning path, track lessons and report progress clearly.

  1. Free diagnostic or consultation.
  2. Matching with a vetted online tutor.
  3. Scheduled virtual lessons and LMS support.
  4. Assignments, CBT practice and lesson tracking.
  5. Parent reports and next-step recommendations.
