Education Delivered With Precision and Purpose

HMG Academy — His Marvellous Grace Academy — was founded in 2015 as a subsidiary of HMG Concepts. What began as a determination to give Nigerian students access to quality education without geographic limitations has grown into a full-service virtual institution serving students from Nursery through to post-secondary level.

We do not work with one teacher. We work with a team — seasoned educators who are specialists in their subject areas, aligned to the Nigerian curriculum, and experienced in preparing students for both local and international examinations.

"Learning Deliberately. Teaching Authentically." — The HMG Academy commitment to every student who walks through our virtual doors.
