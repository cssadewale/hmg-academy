The Founder

Adewale Samson Adeagbo

I started HMG Academy in 2015 from a simple conviction: that Nigerian students deserve teaching that is honest, rigorous, and student-centred. Over a decade later, that conviction hasn't changed — but the tools I use have grown enormously.

Today I am simultaneously an educator and a practising Data Scientist, building machine learning models, creating AI-powered EdTech tools, and documenting the whole journey publicly. CBT Pro — the exam platform powering this academy — was built entirely on an Android tablet. That is the kind of builder I am.

"If you can think through a problem clearly, you can find a solution — even in unfamiliar territory. That is the same lesson I bring to every student I teach."

BSc/Ed Computer Science Education, Lagos State University (2023) · Taught at HMG, Fredaks, Anchor Heights, Dave Model School, Roas Model School, Lifeline, De Glorious, Ennycharles, and more.

Tools & Projects

Every tool here was born from a real classroom problem. Free to use, openly documented.

💻

CBT Pro — Exam Platform

A full computer-based testing system with timed exams, per-student randomisation, AI explanations, auto-marking, and certificate generation. Built in Vanilla JS on an Android tablet. Powered by Supabase.

Open Platform GitHub
Live
📈

Student Performance Tracker

A Streamlit dashboard with 4 interactive tabs — score summaries, at-risk threshold alerts, topic analysis, and question-level breakdowns. Designed for Nigerian secondary school teachers.

GitHub
Live

Student At-Risk Predictor

Random Forest + SHAP model trained on Nigerian secondary school data. Identifies at-risk students before exams — supports single-student and batch CSV modes.
