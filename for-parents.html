For Parents

How to support your child's success in our strictly virtual programs.

Stay Involved

  • • Use the Parent Dashboard Demo to track progress in real time.
  • • Receive weekly reports with strengths, weak areas, and actionable recommendations.
  • • Join live sessions or review recordings (shared via WhatsApp).

Create the Right Environment

  • • Dedicated quiet space for virtual classes.
  • • Consistent schedule aligned with our adaptive plans.
  • • Encourage use of free LMS platforms (Junior/Senior Class, etc.) for practice.

Leverage Free Tools

Our CBT Pro for mocks, Study Plan Generator, and 11 LMS platforms are free for enrolled students. No extra cost for core learning.

Communication

Direct WhatsApp channel to tutors and support team. Quick responses. Share concerns early for adjustments.
