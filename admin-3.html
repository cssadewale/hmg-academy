Google Search Console Setup Guide

  1. Go to search.google.com/search-console → and sign in with Google
  2. Click "Add Property" → URL prefix → enter https://hmgacademy.pages.dev
  3. Verify ownership using the HTML tag method — add the meta tag to your <head> on index.html
  4. Click "Sitemaps" in the left menu and submit: https://hmgacademy.pages.dev/sitemap.xml
  5. Wait 24–72 hours for Google to crawl your site
  6. Monitor "Coverage" and "Performance" tabs weekly
