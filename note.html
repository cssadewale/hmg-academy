Why Free Notes?

Knowledge Shouldn't Have a Paywall

I've been in enough classrooms to know that access to good study material is often the difference between a student passing and failing. Every note on this page was written from scratch — not copied from a textbook, not generated blindly. They reflect a decade of teaching the same topics, understanding where students get stuck, and writing explanations that actually work.

If you find them useful, share them. That's the whole point.
