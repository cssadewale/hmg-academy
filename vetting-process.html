Tutor Vetting Process

Only the top 1% join our virtual tutor network. Rigorous, multi-stage process.

Stage 1: Subject Mastery Exam

Tutors must pass comprehensive tests in every subject they wish to teach. Only those demonstrating deep knowledge advance.

Stage 2: Teaching Interview

In-depth interview covering teaching methodology, patience, communication, and ability to adapt to different learner needs (Nigerian curriculum focus).

Stage 3: Background & Stability Check

Full checks for mental, physical, and social stability to ensure safe, reliable virtual interactions with students.

Stage 4: Ongoing Monitoring

Continuous evaluation through parent feedback, progress data from our LMS and CBT Pro, and our ML tools for early issue detection. Regular reviews.
